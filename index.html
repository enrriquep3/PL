Say hello
What is Project Libros?

Project Libros is a nonprofit dedicated to expanding access to literature for children in foster care, migrant children, and underserved communities. Our mission is to empower futures, one book at a time.

Where do you work?

Our work focuses on children across the United States and Latin America. Our next project supports an orphanage in Oaxaca, Mexico, with books and educational materials.

What kinds of donations can I make?

We welcome financial gifts and donations of new or gently used books. Financial gifts help purchase books, support literacy initiatives, and cover operating costs. Please contact us before sending books so we can coordinate the details.

How can I volunteer?

We welcome people who share our passion for literacy. Email us with a little about yourself and how you’d like to help, or connect with us on Instagram.

How will my donation be used?

Donated funds help us purchase books, cover operational expenses, and support initiatives that promote literacy among children facing barriers to access.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Project Libros is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law.

Can I recommend a community to support?

Absolutely. We welcome suggestions for communities and organizations that could benefit from access to books and learning resources. Send us your recommendation so we can learn more.

How can I stay in the loop?

Follow our journey on Instagram, Facebook, or LinkedIn for updates and ways to get involved.
